# Trend Radar Decision Framework

Reference doc for evaluating trends surfaced from TikTok Creative Center, Google Trends, Reddit, and X. Paste this into a Claude session (or reference it) when doing trend screenshot analysis.

---

## Part 0: Recency Gate (hard cutoff, applied before anything else)

Because this feeds a weekly longlist and a daily meeting, a trend needs to still have room to run when the team acts on it. Age cutoff:
- **Ideal**: originated within the last week
- **Acceptable**: originated within the last 2-3 weeks
- **Fails the gate**: older than 3 weeks, even if volume/likes are still high. High historical numbers on an old trend just mean it already peaked; treat these as "case study" material, not "pitch this" material.

Check actual post dates on example videos (not just the trend's overall view count) before anything else in the triage below.

## Part 1: Trend Triage (four filters, in order)

Apply to every trend that clears the recency gate, before it's considered for the Monday longlist or daily share.

1. **Recency check (secondary, within-trend)** - even after a trend clears the gate above, is the specific content genuinely new, or is a trending term resurfacing old clips? Check post dates in screenshots. If comparing two pulls to check momentum, they must be spaced at least 24 hours apart; same-day pulls tell you nothing about direction.

2. **Org-fit check** - does this require a specific game, IP, or production capability, and does that team have both mandate and bandwidth to move fast?
   - Gaming team: only moves on globally massive games with a global audience. Won't test new/niche games without Jimmy's direct buy-in, regardless of how fast the trend is moving. Default to skip unless it's already a top-tier title the team covers.

3. **Channel-routing check** - main channel, MrBeast Labs (Jake), gaming, or skip. Doesn't kill a trend, just routes it.

4. **Depth check** - does it have genuine relatability, shareability, and psychological pull, or is it surface mimicry (creators copying each other's aesthetic with nothing underneath, likely to fade fast)? Requires watching 2-3 example videos; can't be judged from a trend name and volume chart alone.

**Tiering after triage:**
- Tier 1: passes all four, real format worth Beastifying
- Tier 2: real cultural moment but reactive/tied to someone else's IP, not evergreen
- Tier 3: pure news-cycle chatter, no repeatable mechanic, skip by default

## Part 2: Trend Escalation Score (for "is this big enough to override format preferences")

Use this specifically when a trend is good but doesn't fit Jimmy's natural format preferences (e.g., dance trends - reads as "cringe" for his personality, generally a no). This determines whether it's crossed into corn-kid/Brazil-kid territory, rare enough to justify a format exception and creator outreach.

**Gate (must already be true):** sustained volume over 1-2 weeks, majority of posts recent, genuine outlier views (millions+). If this isn't true, stop, it's not in consideration.

**Weighted signals (score each 0 or 1):**
1. Independent brand pickup - has any brand referenced it unprompted, no outreach involved?
2. Established-creator remix/escalation - did a creator with their own existing following turn it into something bigger (a song, a format others build on)?
3. Mainstream/legacy media pickup - covered by outlets outside social-media trade press?
4. Cross-platform spread - appearing organically on IG/YouTube/X, not just TikTok?
5. Velocity - did it go from posted to tens of millions of views / hundreds of thousands of remixes within roughly 2 weeks?

**Decision bands:**
- 0-1 signals: normal viral trend. Fine for team ideation, not a format-override case.
- 2-3 signals: genuine escalation, monitor daily.
- 4+ signals: corn-kid tier. Creator outreach becomes worth pursuing.

**Precedent case (Corn Kid, 2022):** original clip posted, established remix creator (@schmoyoho) escalated it into a song within 2 weeks (70M+ views, 900K+ uses), brands (Dunkin, Chipotle, JBL) referenced it unprompted within days, mainstream/talk-show pickup within a month. MrBeast brought the creator (Tariq) to a restaurant opening during that window. This is the calibration bar signals 1-5 are checking for.

**Note on data sources:** signals 1, 3, and 4 (brand pickup, media coverage, cross-platform spread) can be checked via web search. Signals 2 and 5 (creator remix, native velocity) require direct TikTok observation, they're not visible from outside the platform.
